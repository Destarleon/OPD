Scores an adult patient's vital signs with the Triage Early Warning Score (TEWS) from the South African Triage Scale, then saves the record for later search. Educational build — not for clinical use. The full SATS also uses clinical sign lists and extra checks (such as blood glucose) that this page does not include.

The ranges come from the adult TEWS in the SATS training manual; the points per range follow that chart's column layout.
